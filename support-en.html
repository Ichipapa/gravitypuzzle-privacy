FAQ

How do I play?

Use the arrow buttons or swipe on the screen to change the direction of gravity. The ball keeps sliding until it hits a wall. Avoid the spikes and get the ball into the green goal to clear the stage.

I'm out of lives

You lose a life when you hit a spike, and you get one back every 20 minutes. On the "Out of Lives" screen, you can watch a video to get a life back right away. With Premium, lives are unlimited.

How do hints work?

Tap the 💡 button on the game screen to see the next 3 moves of the shortest route from your current position, and the button to press next lights up. Stages cleared with a hint get at most 2 stars.
You start with 1 free hint. When you run out, you can watch a video to get one, or buy more in the Shop.

Premium stopped working after I changed devices

Tap the 👑 button at the top right of the stage list to open the Shop, then tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
* Stage progress and hints are stored on the device and are not transferred to a new device.

How do I remove ads?

Buying Premium (a one-time purchase) removes ads.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please use Apple's "Report a Problem" page.
